Sprout 0.1.1

  • Runs on Intel Macs as well as Apple silicon.
  • Runs on macOS 26. The engine in 0.1.0 needed macOS 27.
  • Updates itself: Sprout checks once a day, and Sprout › Check for Updates… checks now.
  • A new app icon, and the About window lists the included software and its licences.
  • Deleted files go to the Trash, with a switch in Settings › Downloads.
  • Fixes from hands-on testing: readable selected rows, ⌘F for the filter, a steadier list, clearer settings and dialogs.
