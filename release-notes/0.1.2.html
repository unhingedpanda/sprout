Sprout 0.1.2

  • More colour, each with one meaning: orange while data comes in, green while it goes out, amber while waiting or checking, red for errors.
  • A finished download blooms: a green sweep through its progress dots, and DONE for a moment.
  • The Dock icon's seedling grows as your downloads progress, and flowers when one finishes.
  • A leaf marks torrents that have shared back as much as they downloaded.
  • Haptic feedback on Force Touch trackpads when you drop a torrent or switch to slower speeds.
  • A couple of hidden surprises in the dots.
